# Episode 1 Storyboard and Action Sheet

Project: `OPAIJA: Staff of Battle`  
Season: `Blood of the Gayelle`  
Episode: `01 - The First Pulse`  
Status: Production handoff draft  
Primary use: storyboard art, Seedance reference frames, Remotion edit, voice narration, QC

## Global Continuity Locks

- Use approved Opaija model sheets for all ten Priority 1 characters.
- Style: 2D Caribbean anime model-sheet style, rounded chins, full lips, broad African/Caribbean nose language, clean black ink, bright island energy.
- Palette: black/charcoal base with gold, orange, red, teal, cream, sea blue, and controlled purple/black for Stillwater scenes.
- Effects must never hide eyes, hands, weapon contact points, or silhouette.
- No generated text inside frames.
- No reference-sheet layout, grids, labels, or multiple pose charts in cinematic frames.
- Seedance references should be clean storyboard production frames, not raw character bible sheets.
